           10.2 Bentley Quarry Road Contributions

           MOTION moved that Council make a submission to the proposed Bentley Quarry
           Development Application outlining the impact on the Kyogle to Lismore road of 100 loaded
           truck movements a day and the contributions needed to adequately maintain the Lismore
           section of this road.

           (Councillors Ekins/Bird)

           AMENDMENT moved that:

              1. Council formally endorses the Lismore City Council submission to Richmond Valley
                 Council regarding the Bentley Quarry regarding the significant impact projected
                 truck movements would have on Council’s road network.
              2. as outlined in the LCC submission that Council reinforces the opinion of staff that of
                 all levies collected from the quarry, 55% of the collected funds should be directed to
                 Lismore City Council, for which these levies can be used in a litigable manner to
                 maintain the roads (primarily Kyogle Road) within our Local Government Area,
                 which will assist in negating the impact of the Quarry on the ratepayers of Lismore.
              3. the General Manager write to RVC to inform them of this resolution.
