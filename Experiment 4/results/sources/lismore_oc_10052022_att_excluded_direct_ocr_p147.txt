March 2022 Quarterly Budget Review Statement Attachment 1
Lismore City Council Quarterly Budget Review Statement
for the period 01/01/2022 to 31/03/2022
Responsible Accounting Officer's Statement
The following statement is made in accordance with Clause 203(2) of the Local Government (General) Regulations
2005: 31 March 2022.
The Quarterly Budget Review Statement for Lismore City Council for the quarter ended 31/03/2022 indicates that
Council’s projected financial position at 30/06/2022 will be satisfactory at year end, having regard to the projected
estimates of income and expenditure as contained in the original adopted budget forecast.
Council's Net Operating Result before Capital Grants and Contributions indicates that Council is using more
resources than it is generating in income. The Operating Deficit before Capital Grants & Contributions is
considered unsustainable and action should be taken to correct as part of the development of Council's Long Term
Financial Plan.
Lismore LGA was hit by catastrophic floods and storms across February, March and April 2022. Extensive damage
was sustained by Council assets staff, suppliers, tenants and contractors and Council facilities were closed due to
staff shortages, change of use (such as the evacuation centre at GSAC) and damage to facilities. While the cash
position at 31 March 2022 appears favourable, it is recommended that all surplus is reserved for flood recovery
works in anticipation of funding delays and to cover assets not otherwise insured or grant-funded.
Long-Term Financial Plan
Consistent with the requirements of the Integrated Planning and Reporting Framework, Council has been working
to finalise a Long Term Financial Plan setting out Council’s projected income and expenditure across a 10 year time
horizon.
Flood recovery works are expected to take several years to complete and will need to be included in the Long
Term Financial Plan. State and Federal Government organisations have pledged to support large portions of the
recovery work and insurance will cover some of the damage to property however rebuilding is expected to take
several years and will be hampered by lack of access to contractors and materials.
In regards to the statement on Contracts and Consultancy, the content is based on information
provided at the time of preparing the March 2022 Quarterly Budget Review Statement.
No assessment of the accuracy of this information was undertaken.
