           14.1 DA21/132 - To undertake a change of use from a service station to a
           neighbourhood shop comprising internal fitout, car parking, landscaping, and
           associated works.

           MOTION moved that Development Application No. 2021/132 for the Change of Use from a
           Service Station to a neighbourhood shop comprising an internal fitout, car parking,
           landscaping and associated works be refused for the following reasons:

              1. the likely impacts of the development on the locality, as it has not been adequately
                 demonstrated that the proposed neighborhood shop will not have an adverse effect
                 on the road network, and or the amenity of the area;
              2. insufficient information has been provided in terms of likely social and community
                 impact (EP&A Act Cl 4.15), inclusive of the impacts from the sale of alcohol:

              3. the site is not suitable for the proposed development and therefore does not
                 comply with Cl 4.15, of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979; and
