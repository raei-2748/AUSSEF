operation 2025-26 North Queensland Monsoon Trough, Associated TC Koji and Severe Weather - recovery operation On this page: Event summary The North Queensland Monsoon Trough, Associated Tropical Cyclone Koji and Severe Weather commencing 24 December 2025 event has impacted many Queensland communities and primary producers across North and North West Queensland. The Queensland Government has moved to ensure flood-affected communities get the help they need through the joint State-Commonwealth Disaster Recovery Funding Arrangements (DRFA). Activation summary In response to this event, assistance has been activated for impacted communities. The activation summary provides an up-to-date view of funding available within impacted LGAs. Financial assistance and exceptional circumstances 2025-26 North Queensland Monsoon Trough, Associated TC Koji and Severe Weather - financial assistance Details about and the standard Disaster Recovery Funding Arrangements (DRFA) funding assistance to support recovery from the 2025-26 North Queensland Monsoon Trough, Associated TC Koji and Severe Weather event, are outlined on the QRA Activations web page. 2025-26 North Queensland Monsoon Trough - exceptional circumstances Jointly funded DRFA exceptional circumstances packages to support recovery from the 2025-26 North Queensland Monsoon Trough event. Recovery operation The recovery operation for this event will include: preparation of a business case for extraordinary assistance under the DRFA offers of support from QRA Recovery and Resilience Officers (RROs) to eligible councils QRA as lead recovery agency liaising with Functional Recovery and Resilience Groups to ensure appropriate state recovery planning. Community Recovery Hotline For assistance with disaster recovery funding eligibility and support please contact the Queensland Government Community Recovery Hotline on 1800 173 349 .
