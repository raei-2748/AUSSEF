exceptional circumstances package On this page: About the DRFA exceptional circumstances package The Tropical Cyclone Jasper recovery operation includes a suite of exceptional circumstances funding packages has been developed to support communities, families, small businesses, primary producers and not-for-profit organisations severely impacted by the Tropical Cyclone Jasper, 13 - 28 December 2023 event. Refer to the DRFA activations summary for this event for the details of the DRFA measures activated for each council. The following DRFA exceptional circumstances package components have been announced: Economic Small Business Support Package - TC Jasper $8.88 million for one-on-one case management focused on mental health and financial security, partnerships with councils and chambers of commerce to address local support needs, and grants for small businesses. Rural Landholder Recovery Grants - TC Jasper $3 million Rural Landholder Recovery Grants Program to support the extraordinary clean-up and reinstatement of rural residential properties that do not meet the criteria for primary producer assistance. Local Recovery and Resilience Grants - $9 million Total $9 million for Local Recovery and Resilience Grants for nine councils following Tropical Cyclone Jasper, 13 - 28 December 2023. Extraordinary Recovery Grants for Primary Producers Extraordinary Recovery Grants for Primary Producers following Tropical Cyclone Jasper, 13 - 28 December 2023. Extraordinary Recovery Grants for Small Business and Not-For-Profits Extraordinary Recovery Grants for Small Business and Not-for-Profits following Tropical Cyclone Jasper, 13 - 28 December 2023. Tourism Recovery and Resilience Program - $29.15 million $29.15 million Tourism Recovery and Resilience Program following the Tropical Cyclone Jasper, 13 - 28 December 2023 event. Cyclone Industry Recovery and Resilience Officers $2.25 million for the Department of Agriculture and Fisheries to support primary producers to recover following the Tropical Cyclone Jasper. Recovery Project Managers - Douglas & Wujal Wujal Funding for Douglas Shire Council and Wujal Wujal Aboriginal Shire Council to engage Recovery Project Managers following TC Jasper. Water and Sewerage Infrastructure Package $61.215 million Water and Sewerage Infrastructure Package available to eligible local government areas in Queensland impacted by TC Jasper. Human and Social Community Health and Wellbeing - TC Jasper $22.94 million Community Health and Wellbeing, Community Recovery and Resilience Officers, Flexible Funding Grants package following TC Jasper and SEQ Severe Storms. Mental Health Program - TC Jasper $9.56 million Mental Health Program  to provide individual and family therapeutic interventions for impacted people following TC Jasper and the SEQ Severe Storms. Wujual Wujal Targeted Assistance package $13.931 million Wujal Wujal Targeted Assistance Package recognising the specific damage to this community following TC Jasper. Roads and Transport Betterment Fund $130 million Betterment Fund available to the nine eligible local government areas in Queensland impacted by the TC Jasper. Environment Environment Recovery package - TC Jasper The $42.985 million Environmental Recovery package is for environmental and cultural recovery activities to accelerate recovery across the most heavily impacted areas. Clean-up Program - TC Jasper Funding to nine local governments and state agencies for extraordinary clean-up activities following Tropical Cyclone Jasper. Bloomfield Flood Risk Management Package $7.73 million to undertake key flood risk management activities in the Bloomfield River catchment encompassing Cook Shire, Douglas Shire and Wujal Wujal. Building Sport and Recreation Community Facilities The $13.23 million Sport and Recreation Community Facilities Program will help rebuild and repair sport and recreation facilities impacted by TC Jasper. Monitoring and Evaluation Monitoring and Evaluation Monitoring and Evaluation funding to ensure DRFA packages are achieving the intended recovery and resilience objectives following TC Jasper.
