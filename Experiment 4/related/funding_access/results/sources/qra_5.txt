exceptional circumstances package On this page: About the DRFA exceptional circumstances package The 2022-23 Northern and Central Queensland Monsoon and Flooding recovery operation includes a suite of exceptional circumstances funding packages that have been developed to support communities, families, small businesses, primary producers and not-for-profit organisations severely impacted by the Northern and Central Queensland Monsoon and Flooding, 20 December 2022 - 30 April 2023 event. Refer to the DRFA activations summary for this event for the details of the DRFA measures activated for each council. To date the following DRFA exceptional circumstances package components have been announced: Economic Extraordinary Recovery Grants of $75,000 for Primary Producers Extraordinary Recovery Grants of $50,000 for Small Business Extraordinary Recovery Grants of $50,000 for Not-For Profit Organisations Coordinated Livestock Support and Disposal Package - $5.75 million Local Recovery and Resilience Grants - $4.25 million Tourism Recovery and Resilience Program - $1.15 million Livestock and Grazing Recovery Program - $250,000 Human and Social Community Mental Health Package - $1 million Community Recovery and Resilience Officers - $2.22 million Roads and Transport Betterment Fund - $100 million Environment Recovery Clean Up and Invasive Species Management Program - $3 million Biodiversity and Riverine Recovery -
