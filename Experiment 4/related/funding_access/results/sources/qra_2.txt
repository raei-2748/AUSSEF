North Tropical Low - exceptional circumstances package On this page: About the DRFA exceptional circumstances package/s The 2025 North and Far North Tropical Low recovery operation includes a suite of exceptional circumstances funding developed to support eligible communities, families, small businesses, primary producers and not-for-profit organisations severely impacted by the North and Far North Tropical Low, 29 January - 28 February 2025 event. Refer to the DRFA activations summary for the North and Far North Tropical Low, 29 January - 28 February 2025 event for the details of the DRFA measures activated for each council. To date, the following DRFA exceptional circumstances package components have been announced: Environment Community Relief Fund The Community Relief Fund, jointly funded by the Australian and Queensland Governments, is available to impacted councils to undertake essential immediate recovery activities. Environmental Recovery To support the restoration of north Queensland’s unique coastline and land ecosystems Economic Tourism Industry Recovery Support Tourism Industries may be eligible for recovery support including financial assistance for operators who lost income after being cut off. Small Business Industry Support Small Business Support Program will support small business owners recover from the impacts of North and Far North Tropical Low, 29 January – 28 February 2025 event, by focusing on mental health support, financial security and building resilient enterprises. Not-for-profit organisations and small businesses Extraordinary Disaster Assistance may be available for Not-for-Profit Organisations and Small Businesses affected by the North and Far North Tropical Low 29 January - 28 February 2025 event. Primary Production Industry Support $7.1 million to assist the Department of Primary Industries tailor holistic support to primary producers in activated LGAs. Disaster assistance for Primary Producers Extraordinary Disaster Assistance for Primary Producers following the North and Far North Tropical Low 29 January - 28 February 2025 event. Coordinated Emergency Fodder Support Package The Coordinated Emergency Fodder Support Package  supports activities to ensure primary producers have access to emergency fodder crucial for sustaining cattle and mitigating the impacts of flooding and loss of pasture in areas impacted by the North and Far North Tropical Low 29 January - 28 February 2025 event. Rural Landholder Recovery Grants The Rural Landholder Recovery Grant Scheme offers grants of up to $10,000 to help rural landholders pay for the costs of clean-up and reinstatement of a damaged primary production enterprise a result of the North and Far North Tropical Low 29 January - 28 February 2025 event. Water and Sewerage Infrastructure Recovery Available to eligible local government areas in Queensland impacted by the North and Far North Tropical Low to restore essential water and sewerage infrastructure that was damaged by the event and ensure essential services are delivered to the community, accelerating recovery and relieving distress. Monitoring and evaluation Monitoring and Evaluation funding to ensure DRFA packages are achieving the intended recovery and resilience objectives following the North and Far North Tropical Low 2025. Stronger Homes Grant Eligible homeowners can access grants of up to $10,000 for works such as raising essential services like electrical switchboards, hot water systems and air-conditioning, data and electrical points, and help protect parts of their homes from flood damage. Built Sport and Recreational Community Facilities Recovery Program $30 million Sport and Sport and Recreational Community Facilities Recovery Program that will help rebuild and repair sport and recreation facilities impacted by the North and Far North Tropical Low 29 January - 28 February 2025 event. Community and Recreational Assets Recovery Program The $20 million Community and Recreational Assets Recovery Program aims to repair and rebuild affected community and recreational facilities damaged by the North and Far North Tropical Low 29 January - 28 February 2025 event. Betterment Funding $205 million to build a more resilient Bruce Highway and other key state-owned connection roads and assets, allowing them to be built back to a more resilient standard. Human and Social Community Health and Wellbeing Program Funding to provide individual support, community development and emotional wellbeing support, targeting vulnerable populations. Mental Health Disaster Recovery Provides individual and family therapeutic interventions across the continuum of care for people that have been directly impacted by the event, mitigating mental health impacts. Legal Assistance Disaster Relief The $1.425 million Legal Assistance Disaster Relief Package will provide extraordinary disaster response legal assistance services to individuals and communities (small businesses, primary producers and not-for-profit organisations) for matters like insurance claims, tenancy problems and property damage in areas impacted Funding acknowledgement The package is jointly funded by the Australian and Queensland Governments to support Queensland communities in their recovery from the North and Far North Tropical Low, 29 January - 28 February 2025 .
