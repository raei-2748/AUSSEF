Producers Extraordinary Recovery Grants for Primary Producers On this page: About Grants of up to $75,000 were available for affected producers following the Northern and Central Queensland Monsoon and Flooding event ( 20 December 2022 - 30 April 2023 ) to hire or purchase equipment and materials, clean up, remove debris, replace fencing and other costs associated with the recovery process. Eligibility Boulia Shire Council Burke Shire Council Carpentaria Shire Council Cloncurry Shire Council Doomadgee Aboriginal Shire Council Mornington Shire Council Mount Isa City Council Funding acknowledgement This program is jointly funded by the Australian and Queensland Governments to support Queensland communities in their recovery from Northern and Central Queensland Monsoon and Flooding, 20 December 2022 - 30 April 2023. Status Applications closed on Friday 29 December 2023.
