# Draft only — QRA data request (not sent)

Subject: Research request — complete project/application and assessment records for the Queensland 2022 Betterment Fund

Dear Queensland Reconstruction Authority,

I am conducting a research feasibility study of whether councils’ pre-disaster financial and administrative capacity adds information about project approval beyond independently documented project need. This is not a request for an assessment of fairness or a causal allegation.

Could QRA provide a machine-readable extract for **all applications and constituent projects in the 2022 Queensland Betterment Fund, Categories D and E**, including all relevant 2021–22 disaster events and later revisions/reconsiderations? Please include approved, eligible unsuccessful, withdrawn, ineligible, pending/unresolved, duplicate/superseded and never-adjudicated records—not only funded projects. State the extraction date and coverage. Include state-agency records with applicant type so the published universe can be reconciled, although the research population will be local-government applicants only.

The public program page reports 123 submissions from 39 councils and TMR, often with multiple projects. Its successful tables contain 221 asset/works rows. Please explain the relationship among submission, application, asset, project, funding stream, revision and published row, and provide a crosswalk reconciling the reported totals and repeated road labels. Please identify all submitting councils and any boundary/name changes.

Requested linked tables and fields:

1. **Identifiers and scope:** stable application/project ID; parent MARS submission ID; revision/supersession IDs; applicant name and stable council/agency ID; applicant type; project/asset name and ID; location/coordinates; asset/project type and original scope; fund/stream; disaster name, event dates and AGRN where held; links to related REPA submissions and overlapping/duplicate funding.
2. **Eligibility and process:** original submission date, complete-application date, eligibility status and assessment date, eligibility reason and rule/version applied; whether the application was substantively assessed; withdrawal date/reason, including administrative withdrawal and re-lodgement; original, revised and final status; every status-transition date. Distinguish approved, partially approved, eligible unsuccessful, ineligible, withdrawn, pending, superseded and deferred/reconsidered outcomes.
3. **Financial request and decision:** original and revised amount requested; total project cost; separately the base restoration/REPA estimate and incremental Betterment amount; council cash/in-kind co-contribution; GST and price basis; approved amount and date, including partial approvals, variations, later approvals and decommitments. Please distinguish approval from cash paid and unknown amounts from genuine zero.
4. **Assessment:** criteria and score scales/weights/version, criteria-level scores, assessment date, recommendation and recommendation date, approval authority and decision date, reasons for non-approval/partial approval, ranking or funding-batch position if used, overrides and reconsideration records, alternative funding and duplication considerations, and funds available/decision batch. If an applicant was not scored, indicate why rather than assigning zero.
5. **Original need and quality evidence:** documented disaster damage and base reconstruction estimate, assessment/inspection date and author/source; hazard/risk and design standard; options and inaction case; submitted BCR or other cost-benefit measures, component costs/benefits, discount rate and horizon, and any assessor revisions; affected community/users/access benefits; vulnerability/remoteness evidence; project readiness, investigations, consultation, technical feasibility, approvals and deliverability evidence held **before the decision**. Identify what was independently assessed versus asserted by the applicant. Where structured values are unavailable, a redacted original assessment summary or dossier with page references would help.
6. **Timing and provenance:** original document creation/submission dates and public release dates where recorded; version history; field definitions, units, missing-value codes, coverage limitations and whether the extract is complete. Please include guideline and co-contribution rules in force at the time, including Indigenous-council exemptions, and identify decisions affected by subsequent budget changes.

A CSV/Excel register with linked assessment and status-history tables is preferred. We do not seek personal contact details; please redact personal information while retaining stable organisational/project identifiers. Where material cannot be released, please identify which fields are withheld or not held and whether a redacted or aggregated alternative is possible. An approved-only extract will not resolve the central feasibility gap. A staged response could begin with identifiers, eligibility, statuses, dates and amounts, followed by assessment/need information.

We will link eligible council applications to dated Queensland council financial records and hold whole councils out of any predictive evaluation. No analysis will treat applications from the same council as independent, or use missing successful-list entries as unsuccessful labels. Please advise the appropriate administrative access process and any likely costs before processing a paid request.

Thank you.
