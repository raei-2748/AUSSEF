information RTI disclosure log RTI disclosure log About QRA's disclosure log Section 78A of the Right to Information Act 2009 sets out the requirement for agencies to publish a disclosure log of information released under right to information. The disclosure log will be updated as information is released under the RTI process. QRA's disclosure log contains information released from 1 July 2009. It does not necessarily identify all information released under RTI. The disclosure log will be updated as information is released under the RTI process.
