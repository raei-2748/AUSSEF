Response to question taken on notice during the Governance, Energy and Finance Committee
hearing on the Supplementary Appropriation Bill 2024-25, held on Wednesday 19 November 2025
Queensland Treasury


Issues
•    The Governance, Energy and Finance Committee (Committee) conducted a public hearing of the
     Supplementary Appropriation Bill 2024-25 on Wednesday 19 November 2025.

•    During the hearing it was agreed that Queensland Treasury would provide further information to the
     Committee on the following matter:

•    In relation to the additional administered funding provided to the Queensland Fire Department for
     the Queensland Reconstruction Authority, can you provide details of what that funding was for in
     terms of initiatives?


Response – Additional administered funding in 2024-25 for the Queensland Fire Department due to
funding for the Queensland Reconstruction Authority of $285.586 million includes:

•    $282.521 million for the reconstruction of essential public assets and other recovery measures for
     the 13 disaster funding activations in 2023-24.

•    $36.436 million for new DRFA Category C and D extraordinary packages including Environmental
     Recovery, Mental Health, Small Business Support, and Sport and Recreation Community Facilities
     Recovery approved in relation to the 2023-24 activations.

•    Offset by -$33.371 million net reduction in the estimated cost and timing of reconstruction and other
     recovery programs for activations across 2019-20 to 2023-24. This net reduction was after funding
     reallocations were made towards 2022 Betterment ($7.2 million), the Voluntary Home Buy-Back
     program ($4.184 million) and Flood Risk Mitigation ($2.29 million).
