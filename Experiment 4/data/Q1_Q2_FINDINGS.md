Recovered the September2021 and December2021 quarterly attachments from their original meeting archive pages, plus signed November minutes and the half-year operational progress report. Raw PDFs are retained unchanged. Q1 has 14 pages (OCR required); Q2 has 13. ## New findings

- Q2 capital notes13 and16 recognise $300,000 Fitzroy Street toilets and $161,818 Khancoban toilets expenditure. The annual report nevertheless labels those amounts as part of the June-adopted budget. Both B0 observations now carry an explicit conflict flag; they remain recorded as reported, not certified as baseline spending authority. Grant-income recognition and capital-expenditure authorisation are not interchangeable.
- Q1 capital note25 defers $3.5m of Tumut Aerodrome works to the following year because of CASA technical-approval delays. This is a signed expenditure change, not a revised full-year budget, and not evidence that a disaster caused the delay.
- The half-year project list repeats original-budget figures rather than providing complete Q1/Q2 revised levels. Its heading therefore does not fill the missing quarterly cells.

These QBRs provide category totals and selected material project changes. They do not supply a full versioned project ledger. Absence from the material-change list cannot safely mean zero revision. The remaining project-level quarterly gaps now have a more specific explanation: the recovered standard attachments report different grains and incomplete sets of changes.
