# A1. NSW fires 1 Jan 2009 to 31 Dec 2014: scoping notes

Agent A1, 2026-09-29. Slice: real NSW bushfire events earlier than the existing 2015-2025 NSW dataset, plus council-level data feasibility (OLG files, 2016 amalgamations, four-pillar gaps).

Labels: **reported** = a source states the figure; **estimated** = I derived it (method given). Nothing here is simulated. No ACM mastheads and no Wikipedia were used. No data files were downloaded. The harness cached PDFs opened via WebFetch in its own tool-results folder, and some RFS PDFs were read in memory by Python. I installed the pure-Python library `xlrd` into the session scratchpad (not the project) to read the old `.xls` OLG workbooks, and my scratch scripts and overlay outputs are in `.../scratchpad/A1_work/` (session scratchpad, outside the repo).

## 0. Bottom line

1. **One big, real, well-documented candidate exists: the October 2013 NSW fires ("Red October").** RFS reports 216 homes destroyed statewide in the 2013/14 season, all in October 2013, with 195 at Linksview Road (Winmalee/Yellow Rock) and 10 at Mt York Road (Mt Victoria), both in Blue Mountains City. Two deaths. Insurance Council catastrophe CAT135. Natural disaster declared for 9 + 8 + other LGAs (Section 2.2). By my GA-polygon overlay (estimated), five councils have >= 5% of their area burned: Hawkesbury 18.2%, Blue Mountains 14.1%, Muswellbrook 13.5%, Lithgow 8.3%, Port Stephens 7.0%. None reaches 20%.
2. **Second real candidate: January 2013 (Wambelong, Warrumbungle Shire).** 53 homes destroyed (RFS), 57 homes for the whole 7-21 January period, no deaths, ICA CAT132 ($35m insured). Warrumbungle burned share is only about 3-4% (estimated), so it will not add a >= 5% row, but the loss rate is the highest in the window (about 13.8 homes per 1,000 residential rating assessments, estimated).
3. **Third: 17 December 2009 Riverina fires** (Gerogery/Walla Walla in Greater Hume, Ournie-Tooma in Tumbarumba): 11 homes destroyed (RFS Bush Fire Bulletin). Small burned share.
4. **Most other declared bushfire events 2009-2014 are low-loss.** They matter only for the "big burn, near-zero loss" end of the score test: Tenterfield Dec 2009 (7.8% burned, no houses found), Armidale Dumaresq Oct 2012 (9.3%, Macleay River fire, no houses found), Lithgow and Singleton Dec 2013-Feb 2014 (7.7%, 7.0%, no houses found).
5. **Council finance data exist and are usable for 2013 fires.** 152 councils in every OLG year FY2008-09 to FY2014-15. All the FP indicators the project needs exist together only from **FY2012-13** (cash expense cover first appears there). There is a **scale break** between FY2012-13 and FY2013-14 in cash expense cover (median 3.9 to 9.3 months) and own-source revenue (59.6 to 71.4). The existing `olg_wide.parquet` starts at FY2013-14, so FY2012-13 (the natural pre-fire baseline for October 2013) is on disk in raw form but not yet parsed.
6. **Boundaries are mostly clean for the 2013 fire councils.** Blue Mountains, Lithgow, Warrumbungle, Port Stephens, Wollondilly, Wingecarribee, Hawkesbury, Muswellbrook, Singleton, Shoalhaven, Yass Valley, Greater Hume are all >= 99.9% retained in the 2021 LGA layer. Merged: Wyong (to Central Coast), Cooma-Monaro (to Snowy Monaro), Tumbarumba (to Snowy Valleys), Palerang, Harden, Armidale Dumaresq, Great Lakes/Greater Taree/Gloucester.
7. **Hard gap: the social-loss pillar.** Per the brief the DSS payments-by-LGA file starts March 2016, so income-support recipients cannot be computed for any 2009-2014 fire. Business counts (CABEE) start June 2013 per the brief, so a January 2013 fire has no pre-fire CABEE value. The OLG sheets carry a NSW-only "Number of Active Businesses in LGA" for FY2012-13 to FY2014-15 and a SALM unemployment rate at 30 June 2013 as partial substitutes.

### 2.4 Estimated burned share per council (GA polygons over ABS 2015 LGAs)

Method (estimated): read Geoscience Australia national historical bushfire boundaries from `ga_original/Bushfire_Boundaries_Historical.gdb`; kept NSW fires with ignition 2008-2014, area >= 1,000 ha, excluding fire_type "Prescribed Burn" (292 fires >= 1,000 ha before excluding burns); union of footprints per event window; intersected with ABS ASGS 2015 LGA polygons (pre-2016 boundaries) in EPSG:3577; share = overlap area / ABS official LGA area (AREA_SQKM in the ABS 2015 shapefile, 1 km2 = 100 ha). **Scale break (reported medians across 152 councils, computed from the files).** Cash expense cover: 3.92 months (FY2012-13), 9.34 (FY2013-14), 10.50 (FY2014-15). **What the existing parquet has.** `olg_wide.parquet` starts at fy_start 2013 (FY2013-14): 152 councils in FY2013-14 and 2014-15 (150 with ratios in 2014-15), 108 in FY2015-16 (unmerged councils only), 128 in FY2016-17 onward. **Fiscal-year alignment.** OLG years run July to June. C1 (Oct 2013) falls in FY2013-14; C2 (Jan 2013) in FY2012-13; C3 (Dec 2009) in FY2009-10. Consequences:

**Implications for comparing 2013 fires with the LGA-2021-based dataset.**

- For the unmerged councils (which include all the Oct 2013 high-burn and high-loss cases: Blue Mountains, Lithgow, Hawkesbury, Muswellbrook, Port Stephens, Wollondilly, Wingecarribee; and Warrumbungle for Jan 2013) the 2013 unit and the 2021 unit are the same place, so ABS series on 2021-based LGAs can be used as they are.
- For merged councils the fire council is only part of the 2021 unit (Wyong 44% of Central Coast, Cooma-Monaro 34% of Snowy Monaro, Tumbarumba 49% of Snowy Valleys, Harden 26% of Hilltops). Either keep the pre-2016 council as the unit for pre-2016 fires (OLG data are published that way for FY2008-09 to FY2014-15 but the ABS and SALM series on 2021-based or 2025-based LGAs cannot be split back without allocation), or use the successor and accept a diluted fire signal.
- The "same-state councils with no fire" control group for a 2013 fire should be drawn from the 152 councils of that time, not the 128 of today.
- Post-fire windows longer than two fiscal years (beyond FY2014-15) break for merged councils: the FY2015-16 OLG file lists only the 108 unmerged councils, and successors appear from FY2016-17.
- Series keyed to the successor code will hide the fire council's own finances after 2016, but the FY2013-14 and FY2014-15 OLG values exist for every 2013-era council.
