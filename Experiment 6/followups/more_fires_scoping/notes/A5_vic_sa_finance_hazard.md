Notes for Victoria:
- The "Know Your Council 2010-11 to 2013-14 dataset" named in the brief was not found under that name. Know Your Council was launched with the LGPRF (FY2014-15 on). Before that the equivalent sources are V2b (VAGO PDF tables) and V5 (LGV annual reports and KPI workbooks).
- VAGO indicator definitions [V, 2010-11 report and 2023-24 Appendix G]: underlying result = adjusted underlying surplus / adjusted underlying revenue; liquidity = current assets / current liabilities; self-financing = net operating cash flow / underlying revenue (2010-11) or internal financing = net operating cash flow / net capital expenditure (2023-24); indebtedness = non-current liabilities / own-sourced revenue; capital replacement = new-asset capital outlay / depreciation; renewal gap = renewal and upgrade spend / depreciation. VAGO also uses net result margin (net result / total revenue) from FY2019-20 [V].
- LGPRF financial indicators [V, Indicator Workbook 2016-17 contents]: L1 current assets vs current liabilities; L2 unrestricted cash vs current liabilities (range 10 to 300 percent); O1 asset renewal vs depreciation; O2 loans and borrowings vs rates; O3 loans and borrowings repayments vs rates; O4 non-current liabilities vs own-source revenue; OP1 adjusted underlying surplus; also Stability (rates concentration, rates effort) and Sustainable capacity C1 to C6 including own-source revenue per head [S1/S2 names from memory, U]. Later editions redefine O1 as asset renewal and upgrade vs depreciation [I, from the VAGO/ESC wording; not checked in the guide].
- Commonwealth Financial Assistance Grants (FAG) advance-payment timing distorts income in FY2013-14/2014-15 and FY2018-19 to FY2020-21 [V, VAGO 2013-14 summary, SA OLG report, VAGO 2023-24 report]. This affects every council in a state, so the "excess vs median of no-fire councils" design should cancel most of it.

Notes for South Australia:
- The database reports are the only council-by-council SA series I found. Councils in Focus and the OLG summary are built from them [V].
- 2008-09 glossary [V]: Cash and Cash Equivalents includes cash on hand and at bank, deposits and investments with the LGFA, banks and other financial institutions; Other Financial Assets includes securities with government authorities, shares and other investments. So term deposits sit mainly inside "cash and cash equivalents".
- Report 8 in 2008-09 already carries a column for FAG advance payments and an "Adjusted Operating Surplus" [V], which removes the timing distortion.
- Per the SA OLG report, operating income excludes capital grants "specifically received for new/upgraded assets" [V]. This differs from NSW total revenue.
- The SA Auditor-General does not appear to publish council-level statements: nothing found [U].
- LGA SA "Financial Indicators Report" (an annual commissioned analysis) is mentioned in a search snippet only [U].

### E3. Is NVIS 6.0 enough as a fall-back forest share?

- I could not open the NVIS 6.0 documentation (DCCEEW pages timed out; search budget spent), so the national coverage, vintage and category scheme of the MDBA ImageServer are [U] here. NVIS is designed as a national compilation of state mapping, so coverage of Victoria and SA is expected [U].
- [I] For a forest-share hazard it is adequate and has the advantage of being the same input already used for NSW, so shares are comparable across states. It carries no slope, weather or urban-interface information and has one vintage, so it cannot follow pre-fire change; nor does it match the BFPL category logic.
- [I] Preferred additions: Victoria, BMO polygon share (post-2011 caveat) plus NV2005 EVC forest share; SA, BPA High+Medium share (2006-2015 amendments) plus DEW NVIS forest share. Use NVIS 6.0 as the common cross-state check.

### Five-line summary

1. Victoria FP pillar: rebuildable as ratios (adjusted underlying result, liquidity/unrestricted cash, asset renewal, debt repayments) for fire years FY2015-16 and 2019-20 from LGPRF, VAGO and VGC; FY2013-14 and 2008-09 only as six VAGO PDF ratios; cash-expense-cover months and services share are not available before FY2014-15.
2. Victoria X finance: LGPRF and VAGO CSVs give operating-ratio, liquidity and renewal analogues; no infrastructure backlog ratio exists; check whether the VAGO "Financial data" header has cash and revenue lines before promising cash-cover months.
3. Victoria hazard: BPA is single-class and nearly whole-council so it barely discriminates; BMO is usable with a post-2011 caveat; no pre-2011 layer exists (no BPA before 7 Sep 2011; WMO polygons absent), so 2009 fires need NV2005 EVC or NVIS as the hazard input.
4. South Australia: the LGGC Database Reports cover FY1995-96 to FY2023-24, every SA candidate fire year (2004-05, 2014-15, 2015-16, 2019-20) has prior and after years, cash and investments are published, and operating surplus, asset sustainability and (from FY2011-12) function spend are available; no backlog or unrestricted-current ratio.
5. SA hazard: Bushfire Protection Areas (2006-2015 amendments, High/Medium/General, 39 councils) is a genuine pre-fire layer for 2014-15, 2015-16 and 2019-20 but not for 2004-05; Code overlays are 0.9 GB, current-only; SA report files FY2006-07 to 2012-13 carry an "internal use" note.
