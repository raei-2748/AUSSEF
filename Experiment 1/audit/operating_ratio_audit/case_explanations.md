# Case explanations


Selections concern two-year changes. Each entry also reports annual steps. Documentary contributors are not complete causal explanations. PDF page references are 1-based.


## Carrathool — 2022-23 (carrathool_2022)

**mixed causes**. OP -5.84% → 23.98% → 26.35%. Two-year change +32.19 pp. Absolute errors: persistence 32.19, ridge 31.53, tree 30.47 pp.

Operating grants rose from $13.681m in FY2020–21 to $20.510m in FY2021–22 and $21.267m in FY2022–23. Depreciation fell $1.963m in the first transition. Council explicitly links ratio improvement to advance Financial Assistance Grants. This combines real funding, timing and expense changes. The FY2022–23 note separates $1.501m storm/flood operating funding ($7.256m comparative) from capital funding. Advance future-year general/roads FAG allocations total $7.874m. Funding receipt and expense recognition need not coincide.

Sources: carrathool_2022 PDF pp 47,69,111,114 | carrathool_2023 PDF pp 68,119,122. https://carrathool.nsw.gov.au/wp-content/uploads/IPR/Annual-Report/Annual-Report-2021-22-FINAL.pdf | https://carrathool.nsw.gov.au/wp-content/uploads/IPR/Annual-Report/2022-2023-Final-Annual-Report.pdf

## Snowy Monaro Regional — 2022-23 (snowymonaro_2022)

**mixed causes**. OP -30.27% → -0.34% → -2.15%. Two-year change +28.12 pp. Absolute errors: persistence 28.12, ridge 16.06, tree 9.09 pp.
