# Bowen RF amended benchmark V2

28 September 2026. Frozen before V2 fitting, after inspecting V1 and its audit. V2 is an explicitly disclosed correction using the already seen holdout. It is not a newly preregistered experiment or fresh independent confirmation.

Question and target remain unchanged: predict the Drive `master` sheet's continuous DL/IL/FP/SL Y from final physical fire characteristics and earlier regional conditions, then convert the prediction using the original three fixed Y class boundaries. Preserve all 218 observations, source cells, target values, pillar availability and class definition. Do not apply outcome-derived death/home floors or independently fit a classifier. Do not change Y to improve scores.

Use the identical 49 connected groups and outer splits from V1. Primary older training set: 139 rows/25 groups; recent holdout: 79 rows/24 groups. Secondary: five-fold grouped outer CV, each with four-fold grouped inner tuning. No random row split. All training-only filtering, imputation, scaling, encoding and equal-group fitting weights remain as in V1. Recompute baselines with the corrected inputs. No new model family.

## Repairs fixed before fitting

1. Select parameters by pooled equal-group inner MAE: sum(each fold's group MAE times its validation-group count) divided by total validation groups. Save those counts and verify the selection independently after fitting. This replaces V1's unequal per-group weights caused by averaging unequal-sized folds equally.
2. Use the saved, explicit source-based `SOURCE_ELIGIBILITY_REGISTRY.csv` for every one of the 223 codebook X candidates. Include all Census-derived exposure, occupied-dwelling and employment fields irrespective of names. Basic Census release guards: after 27 June 2017 for 2016 vintage and after 28 June 2022 for 2021. Employment: after the conservative verified availability bound of 23 October 2017 for 2016, and 12 October 2022 for 2021. SEIFA: after 27 March 2018 and 27 April 2023. The release day itself is conservatively masked because ignition times are not supplied.
3. Both OSM road-exposure columns use the dated 1 January 2019 extract, confirmed by source code and links; mask all earlier events. Exclude the two RA 2021 remoteness columns because no historical version is verified. Exclude all four 2025 BCARR predictors, whose publication vintage cannot establish historical availability. The existing 2014–2017 TRA reference guard remains 1 January 2018; its release day remains unverified. A 2023 REDS product is allowed only after its full publication year (1 January 2024), a conservative year bound. No backfilling or replacement of original workbook values.
4. RF has 500 trees, squared-error loss, bootstrap enabled, no explicit depth limit, seed 20260928, n_jobs=1. Test minimum leaf sizes **[1,2,3,8,15]** times feature fractions **[0.33,0.7,1.0]**: 15 candidates, including standard deeper-tree settings and V1's regularised alternatives. Inner grouped validation alone selects the settings; all outer models are refitted only on their training rows. Save tree-depth diagnostics for each RF candidate and the selected primary model. Ridge alpha stays [0.1,1,10,100]; constant mean and median stay training-only and group-weighted.
5. Pool grouped-CV pillar sensitivities under the correct split label rather than leaving their old per-fold labels. This is a reporting correction; it does not select models or alter Y.

## Evaluation and interpretation

Retain equal-group MAE as primary, with row MAE/RMSE/R², derived-class accuracy/macro-F1, severe/extreme recall, paired group bootstrap (2,000 draws) and pillar/seen-council sensitivities. Retain V1's diagnostic success threshold: >=5% primary MAE gain versus every baseline, positive lower conditional bootstrap difference bound versus every baseline, and lower pooled grouped-CV MAE than every baseline. A pass here would mean the amended benchmark meets those numerical criteria; it cannot become fresh confirmatory evidence on the reused data. Report uncertainty against ridge even if its point estimate is best.

Descriptive permutation importance is not used for selection. No second V2 grid, subset, target revision, new seed search or further fitting based on V2 outer results. A deeper-tree candidate need not win; actual fitting and saved validation scores must demonstrate that it was tested.

## Verification and remaining limits

Pre-fit checks exercise an unequal-fold counterexample, full registry coverage, missed-column masking, unknown-vintage behaviour, source/target preservation and the deeper-tree grid. Post-fit checks independently reconstruct every inner split/group count and selected candidate, recompute reported metrics, verify saved-model predictions, check actual tree structures and confirm original V1 file hashes are unchanged. Passing these defined checks is not scientific certification.

Y still uses ranks and class thresholds constructed across the full workbook, and averages different available pillars. The study remains a frozen-index retrospective benchmark. Annual/quarterly historical release dates and later revisions, TRA publication day and some environmental reference vintages remain uncertified. Fixed 2021 council boundaries harmonise geography; they are not a time-varying predictor. These limitations remain visible and are not silently solved by the eligibility repairs. Groups may retain cross-year/statewide dependencies. Additional untouched data and comparable target measurement are needed for independent operational or policy validation.

All changes are additive in V2. Record source, runner, registry, eligibility implementation, protocol and split hashes before fitting. Source workbook, synced project references and canonical database remain unchanged. Preserve V1 and the audit.
