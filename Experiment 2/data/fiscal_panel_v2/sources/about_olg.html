About the Office of Local Government

The Office of Local Government is responsible for strengthening and regulating councils in NSW

Our work

The Office of Local Government (OLG) is responsible for strengthening the local Government sector, regulating local councils, and advising the NSW Government on local Government matters.

We support the state’s 128 councils with the delivery of services to local communities. As we work collaboratively with the sector, we focus on:

  • sustainability
  • performance
  • integrity
  • transparency
  • accountability.

If a council is not performing, we have legal powers to intervene.

Who we are

The Office of Local Government – Who we are (PDF, 642 KB) provides an overview of our purpose and priorities.

The OLG sits within the Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure, and is the key adviser to the NSW Government on local Government matters.  

It defines our short and long-term vision, priority goals and the strategies that will help us achieve them.

The OLG is committed to effective collaboration with local councils, county councils, joint organisations and peak sector bodies to help inform the Government’s policy and regulatory frameworks.

In January 2025, the OLG shifted to a new structure which focuses on 3 core functional areas: sector performance, sector development and service delivery.

Strategic groups

Sector development

The role of this group is to:

  • develop the sector’s capability and capacity to serve the people of NSW
  • educate the sector on legislative, regulatory and policy responsibilities and accountabilities
  • administer an array of projects and programs that boost sector capability. 
Sector performance

Strengthening our ability to:

  • monitor, measure, and support the performance of councils
  • intervene when challenges arise
  • enforce compliance with regulatory requirements
  • investigate wrongdoing or misconduct within the sector
Service delivery

Programs and activities, with a focus on: 

  • finance, ICT and business services
  • governance and risk
  • how we engage with our stakeholders
Corporate services

The OLG is also supported by core services within the broader Department, aiding our:

  • parliamentary and Government services
  • legal services and counsel
  • finance advisory services
  • communications and media

How we work

What we do

Our purpose is to build a strong and sustainable local Government sector that delivers effective services and civic leadership to communities across NSW.

We create and uphold a robust governance framework, support councils with advice and training, and intervene when issues arise but only when necessary. The OLG’s intervention and regulatory functions relate to conduct and legislative compliance, and the OLG strives to work collaboratively with the local Government sector to mitigate the need for intervention as much as possible.

We also deliver key Government programs that benefit communities such as companion animal reforms, disaster recovery grants, the apprentices, trainees and cadets program, and funding for open spaces and community development.

Our work is guided by values of integrity, transparency, collaboration, and service to community, ensuring councils are supported to lead with confidence and accountability.

Why it matters

Strong councils are the foundation of healthy communities and functional democracy. The OLG helps ensure councils are efficient, accountable, and resilient, especially in times of challenge.

The how

We have adopted a new structure aligned with NSW Government priorities and sector needs. This structure supports our clear strategic direction to strengthen local Government in NSW.

To learn more about how we support and regulate local councils, read Office of Local Government – Who we are (PDF, 642 KB).

Annual reports and corporate documents

The Office of Local Government produces corporate publications to help inform the community about our work.

Information about the Office of Local Government's operation since 2019-20 can be found at the Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure annual reports.

Prior to 24 February 2014, the Office of Local Government was a division of the Department of Premier and Cabinet and reported as part of that agency.
