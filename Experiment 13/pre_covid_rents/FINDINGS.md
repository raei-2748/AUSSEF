# Experiment 13 / pre-COVID rents: test NOT run

3 Oct 2026. Status: **not run**. No data downloaded, no PRESPEC written, no outcome computed.

## The question
Is the rent rise found after Black Summer (Experiment 12: neighbours +7.0% vs far councils; burned councils +2.2% per
100 homes destroyed) also there after fires before COVID? This would separate a fire effect from the 2020-21 regional
rent boom.

## Answer
**Cannot tell. The test was not run.** The Black Summer rent result stays "suggestive, not proven".

## Why
Rent data on disk start in 2017Q3 (DCJ Rent and Sales Report, by council). A search for official earlier data
(2 Oct 2026; every source logged in bibliography/parts/exp13_pre_covid_rents.csv) found:

1. **DCJ Rent and Sales Report back issues, Mar 2010 - Jun 2017** (30 PDFs, about 36 MB). Rents by council only for
   the Greater Metropolitan Region (Sydney, Central Coast, Hunter, Illawarra, incl. Blue Mountains and Port Stephens).
   Rest of NSW only by larger regions (ABS SA3; older regions before about 2012). No all-bedroom median, only 1-4
   bedroom medians. Would allow a clean test for Blue Mountains Oct 2013 and Port Stephens only; country fires
   (Warrumbungle 2013 and 2017, Tathra 2018) only at region level.
2. **NSW Fair Trading bond records, 17 Jun 2013 - 30 Sep 2016** (Data.NSW, one 58 MB file, over the 40 MB limit).
   Postcode level, so council medians could be built, but no 2-year baseline for the 2013 fires, and it may hold only
   bonds still held in 2016.
3. Fair Trading's current monthly bond files start in 2021; older years only by email request.

Ray decided on 3 Oct 2026 to skip these downloads. With source 1 alone the test would have covered about 3-6 burned
councils, so confidence intervals would have been wide.

## If revisited later
Cleanest option: DCJ back-issue PDFs for Blue Mountains (Oct 2013), Port Stephens and their Sydney-region neighbours,
with fire dates moved 24 months earlier as a placebo. Pre-register first (pattern: Experiment 12/PRESPEC.md + LOCK.txt).
