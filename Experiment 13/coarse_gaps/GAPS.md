# Experiment 13: channels that cannot be measured at fire scale (known gaps)

Written 2 Oct 2026. Plain statement of what is missing and why; nothing here is a result.

| Channel | Finest public data found | Why it is a gap | What would close it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local GDP / gross regional product | None official. ABS publishes GDP for Australia and states only. Council "gross regional product" (economy.id / NIEIR) is a modelled estimate | Modelled GRP spreads state output to councils using jobs and industry mix, so a local fire effect would only appear if it is already in the inputs (jobs, businesses). Not independent evidence | Not used unless Ray decides otherwise; the income, jobs and business channels are the direct measures |
| Under-employment / hours worked | ABS Labour Force Detailed: hours and under-employment by state and capital city / rest of state only; SA4 has modelled labour force status only (cube list checked 2 Oct 2026) | SA4 regions hold 100,000-500,000 people, far larger than fire footprints | None public at small-area level; Census 2021 hours worked (one point in time) only |
| Business entries and exits | ABS CABEE data cube 1 (Australia and states) | SA2 and LGA cubes give only counts (stocks), not entries/exits | Net change in counts by industry is tested instead (businesses channel) |
| Mental health | AIHW Medicare-subsidised mental health services by SA3, annual | SA3s are much larger than fire footprints; not fire-matched | Not tested here |
| Smoke health | NSW HealthStats by local health district | Districts cover hundreds of thousands of people; smoke is regional, not footprint-limited | Not tested here |
